**The pitch, not a feature tour.** You are an AWS Innovation Hub engineer presenting to the
**CEO / CMO of a mid-sized SE-Asia fashion retailer** ("Lumen & Coast"). The prototype —
*Lumen & Coast · CreativeIQ* — is the **proof** inside the pitch. Every line is spoken *to that
executive*, about *their* team, *their* trend windows, *their* unused data. The live demo proves
the claim is real.

**Length:** ~8:00 spoken, inside the 10-min cap. **Arc:** their problem → the stakes → the idea →
live proof → the business case → why now / the ask.
